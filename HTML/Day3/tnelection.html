Election-2026

Tamil Nadu Election is an important democratic process conducted to choose the leaders of the state. People who are above 18 years of age have the right to vote in elections. Elections help citizens select responsible leaders who can work for the welfare and development of society. Different political parties participate in the election and present their plans to the public. During election time, campaigns, rallies, and public meetings are conducted across the state. Voters visit polling booths and cast their votes on election day. After voting, all votes are counted carefully by election officials. The party that wins the majority of seats forms the government. Elections play a major role in strengthening democracy in India. Every citizen should participate honestly and responsibly in the voting process.

Election Process

The election process in Tamil Nadu starts with the announcement of election dates by the Election Commission. Political parties select their candidates for different constituencies. After this, election campaigns begin across the state through rallies, speeches, posters, and media promotions. Candidates explain their promises and development plans to attract voters. On election day, citizens visit polling booths and cast their votes using EVM machines. Election officials monitor the process to ensure fair voting. After voting is completed, all votes are counted carefully at counting centers. The final results are then officially announced. The political party that wins the majority of seats forms the government. This process helps maintain democracy and public participation.

Election result-2026

The 2026 Tamil Nadu Assembly Election created a major political change in the state. Actor-turned-politician Vijay’s party, Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), became the single largest party by winning 108 seats in the 234-member Assembly. DMK won 59 seats, AIADMK secured 47 seats, Congress won 5 seats, and PMK got 4 seats. Even though TVK became the largest party, it initially did not cross the majority mark of 118 seats required to form the government.

Current political situation

The current political situation in Tamil Nadu is very tense and active. TVK is trying to form the government with the support of Congress, CPI, CPI(M), and VCK parties. Reports say that TVK has now crossed the majority mark with alliance support and is close to forming the government under Vijay’s leadership. At the same time, DMK and AIADMK are also holding discussions and political negotiations to stop TVK from gaining power. Several meetings, alliance talks, and government formation discussions are happening across the state. The Governor is also checking whether TVK has enough support to officially form the government.
