My Favourite Musician

Sid Sriram

Description

Sid Sriram is a famous Indian-American singer, songwriter, and music producer known for his soulful voice and emotional singing style. Born on May 19, 1990, in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, he later moved to the United States with his family. He learned Carnatic music from his mother and later studied at Berklee College of Music in Boston. Sid Sriram became popular after singing the song “Adiye” from the Tamil movie Kadal, composed by A. R. Rahman. He is known for combining classical Carnatic music with modern genres like R&B, jazz, and pop. Sid Sriram has sung many hit songs in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, and Hindi films. Some of his most popular songs include “Maruvaarthai,” “Inkem Inkem Inkem Kaavaale,” and “Srivalli.” His emotional voice and unique musical style have made him one of the most loved singers in Indian cinema. Along with playback singing, he also performs live concerts around the world and continues to inspire young music lovers with his talent and dedication.
