Destination Highlights

Kashmir - Heaven on Earth

Kashmir is famous for snow-covered mountains, beautiful valleys, Dal Lake, and traditional Kashmiri culture. Visitors can enjoy shikara rides, trekking, skiing, and delicious Kashmiri cuisine.

Goa - Beach Paradise

Goa is well known for beaches, nightlife, seafood, water sports, and Portuguese architecture. Tourists can enjoy parasailing, scuba diving, beach parties, and sunset views.

Manali - Adventure Destination

Manali offers breathtaking mountain views, river rafting, snow activities, camping, and adventure sports. It is one of the best destinations for honeymoon trips.

Ladakh - Land of High Passes

Ladakh is famous for rugged mountains, monasteries, bike trips, Pangong Lake, and adventurous roads. It is a dream destination for nature lovers and bikers.
